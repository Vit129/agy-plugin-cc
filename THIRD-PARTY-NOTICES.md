## Agent platform icons

Agent Usage renders agent brand marks from vector geometry and tints them at draw time. The SVG geometry and Swift SVG path parser are adapted from [Kouen](https://github.com/Vit129/kouen-terminal) (Apache-2.0).

- **[Lobe Icons](https://github.com/lobehub/lobe-icons)** (MIT, see below), the
  `@lobehub/icons-static-svg` monochrome (`currentColor`) variants: `codex`, `claude`
  (Claude Code), and `githubcopilot` (GitHub Copilot).
- **Vendor brand mark** for `antigravity` (Google Antigravity), traced from the icon asset
  published at antigravity.google/press. A trademark of its owner, embedded only as
  monochrome geometry to identify the running agent — distinct from, not a copy of, Google
  Gemini's mark.

Platform names and logos are trademarks of their respective owners. They are used
here solely to identify the corresponding coding agent in the UI.
